# Early-Game Route Checklist

Use this as a flexible route: complete the steps in order where practical, and return to any deferred activities when convenient. Numbers in parentheses indicate XP/task-point values from the original notes.

## Lumbridge: Getting Started

1. Complete the **Leagues** tutorial and unlock your first relic. (+10 task points)
2. Talk to Hans in Lumbridge Castle and ask how old you are. (+10 task points)
3. Open the Skill Guide. (+10 task points)
4. Use Home Teleport. (+10 task points)
5. Listen to a musician. (+10 task points)
6. Claim a free item from Lumbridge General Store. (+10 task points)
7. Return a free item to Lumbridge General Store. (+10 task points)
8. Sell an item to a store. (+10 task points)

## Thieving and Farming

9. Pickpocket until level 5 Thieving.
10. Steal from the bakery stall 10 times. (+20 task points)
11. Continue stealing from the bakery stall until level 15 Thieving.
12. Visit the H.A.M. Hideout and pickpocket a H.A.M. member.
13. Rake the tree patch behind Lumbridge Castle. (+10 task points)

## Supplies and Basic Combat

14. Buy bait from the fishing store.
15. Buy a bucket, pot, shears, and jug.
16. Fill the bucket and jug with water.
17. Kill a goblin in Lumbridge. (+10 task points)
18. Kill a giant spider. (+10 task points)
19. Kill a chicken. (+10 task points)
20. Milk a cow. (+10 task points)
21. Climb over the stile north of Lumbridge.
22. Kill chickens, cows, or goblins until combat level 10; bury every bone you receive.

## Woodcutting, Fletching, and Firemaking

23. Chop 10 normal trees, completing the Basic Tree and Five Tree tasks. (+30 task points)
24. Continue chopping logs until level 20 Woodcutting.
25. Fletch arrow shafts. (+10 task points)
26. Chop one oak log. (+10 task points)
27. Burn five logs. (+20 task points)
28. Cut a willow tree east of Lumbridge Castle. (+30 task points)

## Prayer and Fishing

29. Activate Thick Skin, Rock Skin, or Steel Skin Prayer. (+10 task points)
30. Run out of Prayer points. (+10 task points)
31. Complete *The Blood Pact*. (+10 task points)
32. Catch 10 shrimp, including the Five Fish task. (+30 task points)
33. Continue catching shrimp until level 15 Fishing and Cooking, dropping the shrimp as needed.
  - If you cannot burn food, use cooked meat or chicken on a fire instead.
34. Catch a herring at level 10 Fishing. (+10 task points)
35. Catch an anchovy at level 15 Fishing. (+10 task points)

## Cooking, Crafting, and Runecrafting

36. Pick a potato from the farm north of Lumbridge, near the windmill.
37. Cook and eat a baked potato. (+10 task points)
38. Pick and eat a cabbage. (+10 task points)
39. Pick and eat an onion near the sheep. (+10 task points)
40. Climb over the stile near the sheep until level 5 Agility. (+10 task points each)
41. Stand one tile from a wall, face the wall, and cast Surge. (+10 task points)
42. Complete the Sheep Shearer miniquest. Return later if it is busy.
43. Shear five additional wool.
44. Pick wheat. (+10 task points)
45. Make flour. (+10 task points)
46. Use a jug of water on the flour.
47. Spin wool in Lumbridge Castle. (+10 task points)
48. Pick five flax. (+10 task points)
49. Go to the house south of Bob's Axes and cook bread on the range. (+10 task points)

## Mining, Smithing, and the Lumbridge Swamp

50. Go to the Lumbridge Mine, south of the graveyard.
51. Prospect any rock. (+10 task points)
52. Mine clay. (+10 task points)
53. Use a bucket of water on clay to make soft clay. (+10 task points)
54. Mine 10 copper ore. (+30 task points)
55. Mine 10 tin ore. (+20 task points)
56. Continue mining until level 10 Mining, dropping excess ore. Leave with at least 10 copper and 10 tin ore.
57. Home Teleport to Lumbridge.
58. Spin five bowstrings. (+10 task points)
59. Go to the smithing building.
60. Smelt 10 bronze bars. (+20 task points)
61. Smith any bronze item. (+10 task points)
62. Return to Lumbridge Swamp.
63. Kill a giant rat and pick up its meat.
64. Cook rat meat on the swamp campfire. (+10 task points)
65. Mine iron ore southwest of Lumbridge Swamp. (+10 task points)
66. Mine coal.
67. Maintain nearly full stamina for 60 seconds. (+10 task points)

## Draynor, Zanaris, and Runespan

68. Enter Zanaris through Lumbridge Swamp. (+30 task points)
69. Leave Zanaris.
70. Go to the Divination spot in Draynor.
71. Gather and convert 10 pale memories. (40)
72. Have Ned make a rope from four balls of wool. (+10 task points)
73. Unlock the Draynor lodestone.
74. Climb to the top of the Wizards' Tower. (+10 task points)
75. Kill the lesser demon. Expect a possible queue. (+10 task points)
76. Siphon from a fire essling in Runespan. (+10 task points)

## Wendlewick and Havenhythe

77. Teleport to Wendlewick.
78. Climb the Wendlewick Lighthouse. (+10 task points)
79. Go to the beach.
80. Kill a juvenile boulder crab. (+10 task points)
81. Use the outhouse north of Wendlewick. (+10 task points)
82. Go to the Goblin Camp deserted mine in southeast Havenhythe.
83. Pick up the burnt lobster behind Frogtongue.
84. Teleport back to Wendlewick.
85. Find and speak to the drunken villager. (+10 task points)
86. Attempt to eat the burnt lobster in the Burnt Lobster Tavern. (+10 task points)
87. Start *Hearts of Sanguine* and progress to the Havensilver mining step.
88. Mine 20 Havensilver. (+10 task points)
89. Smith a Havensilver weapon. (+10 task points)

## Varrock and Edgeville

90. Home Teleport to Lumbridge.
91. Kill five chickens, cows, or goblins and pick up their bones.
92. Travel to Varrock and unlock the lodestone.
93. Kill a dark wizard. (+10 task points)
94. Mine iron ore southwest of Varrock. (+10 task points)
95. Give the stray dog a bone. (+10 task points)
96. Steal from the Varrock tea stall. (+10 task points)
97. Give Elsie a cup of tea above Varrock's north church. (+10 task points)
98. Complete the Varrock Museum quiz for easy Hunter and Slayer levels.
99. Buy and equip a full iron set from Horvik's shop. (+10 task points)
100. Buy and equip a full green dragonhide set from Lowe's Archery Shop and Emporium. (+10 task points)
101. Buy and equip full imp-hide robes from Zaff's Superior Spellcasting. (+10 task points)
102. Buy and equip full spider-silk robes from Zaff's Superior Spellcasting. (+10 task points)
103. Get a clue scroll from Zaida, south of the Grand Exchange. (+10 task points)
104. Go to the Jolly Boar Inn and buy a beer.
105. Kill an imp near the Archaeology Dig Site. (+10 task points)
106. Scatter its ashes. (+10 task points)
107. Pan for an uncut jade at the Dig Site. (+10 task points)
108. Complete the Archaeology tutorial. (+10 task points)

## Fort Forinthry, Edgeville, and Necromancy

109. Harvest 10 pale memories. (+20 task points)
110. Complete *New Foundations*. (+30 task points)
111. Use the Fort Forinthry workshop bank. (+10 task points)
112. Make a plank at the Fort Forinthry sawmill. (+10 task points)
113. Give Bill a beer at Fort Forinthry. (+10 task points)
114. Offer five bones at an altar. (+10 task points)
115. Go to Edgeville and unlock the lodestone.
116. Browse Oziach's shop north of the lodestone. (+30 task points)
117. Kill the mugger near the lodestone. (+10 task points)
118. Mine coal in Barbarian Village. (+10 task points)
119. Complete the Stronghold of Player Safety. (+30 task points)
120. Go to the Draynor lodestone.
121. Start and complete *Necromancy!*. (+10 task points)
122. Complete a lesser necroplasm ritual. (+10 task points)
